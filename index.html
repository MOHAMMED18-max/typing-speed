About Typing Speed

Typing Speed is a lightweight typing test platform designed to help users measure and improve their typing speed, accuracy, and consistency.

Built with performance in mind, it runs entirely in your browser — no account, no backend, no tracking. Your results are stored locally on your device, so your data stays with you. Whether you are preparing for professional work, studying, or simply refining a daily skill, Typing Speed offers a focused, distraction-free environment to practice and progress.

  • 200 long-form passages across five difficulty levels — 40 per level, 300–900 words each, including public-domain classics and original writing
  • Accurate WPM and accuracy calculations
  • Local personal best and test history
  • Lightweight, responsive, and accessible

“Consistency beats intensity. Small, regular practice is the fastest path to lasting improvement.”
